# Customer-support Figure

One interactive Anatomy Figure: a support operations workbench. A filing cabinet represents LightRAG and custom ingestion/retrieval; a patch panel represents the central MCP server and legacy integrations; a conversation terminal represents the chatbot; an operator terminal represents DSM. A signal travelling across the bench means a conceptual request moving between components, not live traffic. Physical appearance is illustrative, not a depiction of company equipment.

Parts: four feet, base plate, inset rim; cabinet plinth, housing, three drawers, three handles, three document trays; MCP plinth, enclosure, three removable panels, six connector wells, three indicator lamps; two terminal feet, two pedestals, two monitor housings, two glass screens, six screen content marks, two keyboard trays and 24 keys. Every solid mounts on another solid. Etched closed tracks sit on the bench surface. No cylindrical solids, cables or pipework. Depth-sort assemblies by camera depth, draw their attached details in face order.

World: x/y bench surface, z up; rectangular plans, 45° azimuth, 32° elevation, fitted to 960×590. Four-tone warm neutral finish, copper active assembly. Static geometry rendered at build time using vendored MIT Anatomy kit. GSAP's existing ticker drives the optional tour; offscreen/document hidden pauses it, reduced motion is static. Buttons, scenario selector and next-step control work with keyboard. All explanations remain readable without JavaScript.

Evidence: owner reports approximately 40,000 conversations/month, separately from design capacity of up to 500,000 queries/day. Customer-ended threads are included in without-agent; do not call this verified resolution. The department mix may change the total rate. Raw company report, exact internal volumes, customer data, backend addresses and commercial forecasts are not published.
